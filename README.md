# Microinteractions

Three CSS-only studies in button feedback, parent-triggered orientation, and
looping animation, with keyboard focus and reduced-motion alternatives.

Open [index.html](./index.html) locally or visit the
[GitHub Pages site](https://chrisalves210.github.io/microinteractions/).
Use Tab to explore focus states and enable your system's reduced-motion setting
to see the alternatives.

State matrices and motion decisions are in [DESIGN-LOG.md](./DESIGN-LOG.md).
